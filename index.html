    Our story

    Protégé Power was founded in 2015, born from the need for affordable, reliable energy solutions in South Africa. With electricity prices climbing and the grid becoming less dependable, we set out to design solar systems that cut costs and give homes and businesses true independence from the grid. Since then we have grown into a trusted name in the solar and backup power industry, helping hundreds of clients take control of their energy future.

    We design and install quality renewable energy solutions to a high standard of workmanship, using only the best components available and keeping your budget in mind.

    Our offices are in Pretoria East and we operate anywhere in Gauteng and the neighbouring provinces.

    Common questions

    What benefit would I get from a solar system?

    A solar system gives you three main benefits. Cost savings: you generate your own power and cut your electricity bill. Electricity backup: your essentials keep running during power outages. Eco-friendly energy: you use clean power from the sun and reduce your carbon footprint.

    What benefit would I get from a solar system?

    A solar system brings three main benefits. Cost savings: it cuts what you pay for electricity, by up to 95% depending on the system. Electricity backup: your essentials keep running when the grid goes down. Eco-friendly power: you generate clean energy from the sun and reduce your carbon footprint.

    Do I need a site visit before I get a final quote?

    No two properties are the same. Roof layout, your electrical setup and how you use power all change the design, so every system is designed individually. We do not visit every site, but we always discuss your usage and priorities in detail before we design.

    Do you offer lithium batteries?

    Yes. We offer the latest lithium battery technology and will recommend the battery that best suits your usage, priorities and budget. Your quote confirms exactly which battery is included.

    Do you install commercial and industrial systems?

    Yes. These start with a site assessment and a detailed, accurate proposal. Use the contact form and choose commercial.

    Which areas do you cover?

    Our offices are in Pretoria East and we operate anywhere in Gauteng and the neighbouring provinces.
